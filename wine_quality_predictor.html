Model Architecture

Gradient Boosting Classifier (GBC) trained with 200 estimators, learning rate 0.1, max depth 4. Uses ensemble of decision trees for robust predictions.

Quality Classification

  • Low (3–4) — Below average wine with notable flaws
  • Medium (5–6) — Standard commercial quality
  • High (7–9) — Premium wine with excellent characteristics

Key Predictors

Alcohol content is the strongest predictor (28%), followed by volatile acidity (15%) and sulphates (15%). Lower volatile acidity and higher alcohol/sulphates correlate with higher quality.

Data & Training

Trained on 2,000 physicochemically-generated wine samples with feature engineering based on the UCI Wine Quality dataset properties. 80/20 train/test split with 5-fold cross-validation.
